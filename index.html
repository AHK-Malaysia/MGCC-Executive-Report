The Big Picture

🇲🇾

Economy Accelerating

Malaysia's economy continues to develop positively, driven by robust private consumption (60% of GDP). Analysts expect 5% GDP growth for 2024 vs 3.7% in 2023.

✓ Labour market positive
🤝

Top ASEAN Partner

Malaysia remains Germany's most important trading partner within ASEAN. Almost 50% of goods sourced are electronic products, primarily semiconductors.
