Visa: A Policy Shake-Up is Coming

The Ministry of Home Affairs has announced a major revision to the Employment Pass salary framework effective 1 June 2026, aligned with the Thirteenth Malaysia Plan's goal to prioritise local talent development.

All new and renewal EP applications submitted on or after 1 June 2026 must comply with revised thresholds. Companies are strongly encouraged to review their workforce planning and compliance strategies now — before the deadline.

Why China Fairs Surged

Visa-free travel via Malaysia-China exemption agreements removed friction. China remains Malaysia's largest trading partner for over a decade, and companies are actively sourcing cost-effective materials from Chinese suppliers to optimise supply chains.

🇩🇪

Why Germany Fairs Grew

Germany is Malaysia's largest EU trading partner. Malaysian companies flock to German fairs to source advanced machinery, automotive components, electronics, and renewable energy technologies unavailable elsewhere.

🌟 NRW ASEAN Service Desk

On 5 December, MGCC hosted a webinar on "Unlocking Growth in Germany's Food Industry: Business Opportunities in NRW" — partnering with AHK Thailand and AHK Vietnam. Nearly 100 participants from Malaysia, Vietnam, Singapore, and Indonesia joined.
