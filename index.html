Section C · Visa Services

Visa & Corporate Services

Revised Expatriate Salary Policy & Employment Duration Framework — Effective 1 June 2026

The Ministry of Home Affairs (MOHA) has announced that a revised expatriate salary policy will take effect on 1 June 2026. The revised policy is aligned with the objectives of the Thirteenth Malaysia Plan (RMK-13) to reduce reliance on foreign labour and to prioritise the employment and development of suitably qualified Malaysian talent. Under the revised framework, minimum salary thresholds for Employment Pass (EP) Categories I, II and III have been updated, and a structured employment duration of 10 years has been introduced. These measures are intended to support sustainable economic growth, enhance skills transfer, and strengthen the development of Malaysia's local workforce. Organizations employing expatriates are encouraged to review their workforce planning and compliance arrangements in anticipation of the effective date. Further guidance will be issued by the relevant authorities in due course.

🇩🇪 Possible Factors Contributing to Increased Trade Fair Participation

Visitor numbers — particularly for German fairs — have risen significantly in 2025. Key contributing factors include:

1. Strong Trade Relationship: Germany is Malaysia's largest trading partner in the EU, especially in machinery, automotive, chemicals, and precision engineering. This deep commercial tie creates sustained interest in German trade fairs as primary sourcing and networking platforms.

2. High-Value Sectors: Malaysian companies visit Germany to source advanced machinery, automotive parts, electronics, and renewable energy technologies — sectors well represented at events like bau, productronica, and Transport logistic.

🛡️ IT Security & Strategies

Between October and December 2025, Microsoft Teams received a series of updates focused on enhancing collaboration, productivity, and security. Key improvements included enhanced chat organisation, AI-powered Copilot capabilities for generating meeting summaries and suggesting messages, pop-out windows to support multitasking, strengthened access controls for private teams, and upgraded meeting features such as live language detection and enhanced town hall functionality. Collectively, these enhancements were designed to streamline communication, improve meeting efficiency, and reinforce secure teamwork.
During Q4 2025, Windows 11 and Windows Server received their regular monthly security updates. The October release addressed over 170 vulnerabilities, including several critical and zero-day threats. The November and December updates resolved more than 50 vulnerabilities each, including issues that were actively being exploited. These updates play a vital role in safeguarding systems against cyber threats, maintaining operational stability, and ensuring ongoing compliance with security standards.
Several key software subscriptions were renewed to ensure uninterrupted operations, sustained security protection, and continued productivity:
